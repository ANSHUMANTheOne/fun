# My simple and yet practical calculator app which saves history (with a twist :)

Just a basic calculator where you can enter two values, choose an operation, and get the result.

It also keeps a history of your calculations and handles mistakes like 12 / 0 by showing cant divide by 0 instead of breaking the calculator.

The calculator looks simple when you first open it, but there are a few things that change as you use it. Every calculation makes the background more colorful and adds a piece of ASCII art somewhere around the page. Certain numbers such as 1738, 69, 67, 41, and 21 also trigger their own reactions with GIFs, video, and ASCII art.

The idea was to keep the main calculator simple while making the experience slowly change the more you use it.

Hope you enjoy the simplistic by design and slightly ridiculous calculator website

(Just as revolutionary as when Tim Cook released the Calculator app on ipad :D )

Notes:-
The website was designed to stay simple and clean instead of filling the screen with unnecessary colors and effects.

The background changes gradually with each successful calculation.

ASCII art appears in different places as more calculations are made.

Some specific numbers trigger special reactions.

Calculation history can be cleared with the Clear button.

The layout and alignment were adjusted with help from ChatGPT, including debugging some parts of the js and fixing why the result was not appearing.

The ASCII art used in the project was sourced from https://emojicombos.com/ascii-art
